زهرة

دورتك وصلاتك بوضوح · Masive Tech

Zuhra

Your cycle and your prayers, clearly · Masive Tech

Zuhra is a period tracker for Muslim women. Every day it tells you, in plain words, whether you can pray and fast today: tahir, hayd, istihada or nifas. It keeps the fasts you owe from Ramadan, and it shows you the reason behind every state.

Support

Something not working, or a question? Write to mohamaachour@gmail.com and describe what happened. Every message is read.

Common questions

  • Which school does it follow? Zuhra applies one range to everyone: a period of 1 to 15 days, the position of the majority of jurists (Maliki, Shafi'i and Hanbali). The Hanafi range is 3 to 10 days, and the app says so openly. Every ruling in it appears in square brackets with its source.
  • Is this a fatwa? No. Zuhra applies general rules; unusual patterns (very long bleeding, pregnancy, postpartum, miscarriage) are for a scholar.
  • Is it medical advice or contraception? No. Predictions are estimates from your own log and must not be used to avoid pregnancy. For pain or unusual bleeding, see a doctor.
  • Where is my data kept? On your phone only. There is no account and no server, and the app sends nothing over the internet.
  • How do I delete my data? Me › Delete everything on this phone, or delete the app.
